# The Wellbeing Tightrope <small>for anyone Balancing on the Tightrope of Life</small>

The tightrope framework is for anyone facing a number of pressures and responsibilities. Or perhaps you have a goal or dream that requires a change process and this can create a "tightrope effect". 

The outcome of using this framework means ...
- reflecting on your goals and the factors that support you, which increases resilience
- acknowledging past experiences and current pressures to provide insights into why achieving your goal is more difficult
- exploring your qualities and strengths to motivate you to take action towards your goal
- recognising patterns in the behaviours that are unhelpful
- identifying values that are pulling you in both directions and how to focus on those that support your goal

Furthermore, by utilising Emotional Freedom Technique (also known as 'tapping') you can clear limiting beliefs and create a space for healing.

# <small>Emotional Freedom Technique (EFT)
The Wellbeing version of the Tightrope includes Emotional Freedom Technique (EFT) practice. EFT was created by Gary Craig in the 1990s after working with Dr Roger Callahan, who created Thought Field Therapy. Gary describes EFT as a psychological version of accupuncture. 

EFT is also known as 'tapping' because it involves tapping with the finger tips on meridian points around the head, face and upper body to shift your energy system. The process involves talking through the problem until the energey around the problem shifts towrads release. 

Tapping can calm anxiety and stress and can address a range of physical and emotional health concerns. 
EFT is effective at addressing past traumas to support a journey toward recovery. 

An EFT tapping script is included for each chapter of the workbook, along with an image of where the tapping points are on the face and head. 

You can create your own scripts based on any problem or pressure you are facing - keep tapping until you feel a shift or release.

This has been the magic wand for my wellbeing improvement journey!
